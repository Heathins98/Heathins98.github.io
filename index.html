S/4HANA Analysis Tool Suite

Enterprise performance and analysis tooling built inside a Fortune 500 SAP landscape. The suite pulls workload statistics, work-process state and short-dump history out of production systems on a schedule, normalises them into a single store, and gives Basis teams comparative views the standard transactions do not offer: release-over-release response time, batch-window contention and tenant-level growth forecasts.

Used as the reference dataset for load-test sign-off across two major upgrade cycles. The full writeup covers the architecture at the level of components and data flow.
